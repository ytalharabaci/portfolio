Hi, I'm Yusuf Talha. I’m a **Security Engineer** with a computer engineering background, focused on bridging the gap between enterprise security operations and resilient systems engineering.

My experience spans multi-tenant MDR (60+ clients) and high-availability banking CSIRT environments, handling threat detection and incident response across critical infrastructures. Rather than just reacting to alerts, I approach defense with a software engineer’s mindset: building automated detection pipelines, hardening cloud-native workloads (Kubernetes & Docker), and engineering custom security tooling in Go and Python.

Alongside my day-to-day work, I am pursuing an **M.Sc. in Software Engineering** at Karabuk University, focusing my research on open-source software supply chain security and automated threat modeling—while authoring open-source technical web books ([ytalharabaci.dev](https://ytalharabaci.dev)) to share practical SecOps and Network Defense knowledge.

Currently in the thesis stage with all coursework completed—**fully available for full-time Security Engineer, Detection Engineer, and SecOps roles**.

### Core Competencies

- **Security Automation & Tooling:** Go, Python, REST APIs, Custom Tooling & Parsers
- **Detection Engineering:** Detection-as-Code (Sigma, Elastic, Splunk, Correlation Rule Tuning)
- **Operations & DFIR:** Enterprise Incident Response, Threat Hunting, Multi-Tenant MDR/SOC Operations
- **Cloud & Infrastructure:** Kubernetes, Docker, Container Hardening, Linux/Windows Security
