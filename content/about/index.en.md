Hi, I'm Yusuf Talha. I'm a **Security Engineer** focusing on threat detection, incident response, and defensive security automation.

Throughout my career, I've worked in 24/7 operational security environments across banking and enterprise networks. I have hands-on experience managing and monitoring SIEM and EDR/XDR platforms (such as Splunk, Elastic, Cortex XDR, and CrowdStrike Falcon), investigating suspicious activity, handling Tier-2 incident triage, and engineering detection rules.

I believe effective defense requires looking at systems through an attacker's lens. Alongside detection operations, I work on application security (embedding SAST and container scanning into CI/CD pipelines) and infrastructure hardening (securing Linux and Windows servers, network segmentation, and container isolation). I also enjoy automating operational tasks with Python, Go, and Bash, and building open-source security tools.

I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from Bursa Technical University and am currently pursuing an **M.Sc. in Software Engineering** at Karabük University, with research focused on software security and secure coding practices.
